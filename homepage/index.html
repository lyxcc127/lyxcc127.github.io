Biography

I am currently an intern at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Yebin Liu. I received my B.E. from Northeastern University (Shenyang, China) and my M.E. from Huazhong University of Science and Technology, advised by Prof. Delie Ming.

My current work focuses on leveraging diffusion models to enhance 3D reconstruction. My research interests include head avatars, 3D reconstruction, and 3D generation.

I am actively seeking PhD opportunities. If you have openings related to avatars, 3D reconstruction, 3D generation, or 3D scene understanding and interaction, feel free to reach out by email .
